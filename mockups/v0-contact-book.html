Design notes for review (not part of the app)

  1. Paste → Parse → Review → Save. Nothing is written until you press Save, so a bad parse never pollutes the book.
  2. Fast vs AI. The standard email format is parsed by rules (free, instant). AI (Gemini) is the fallback for odd blurbs, and later could run automatically when rules aren't confident.

  4. Duplicate check on Save by phone number: if the number exists, offer "Update existing" instead of a second row.
  5. Fields are a first guess from the sample blurb. Tell me what to add, drop, or rename (e.g. Rep, Lead source, Roof type, Storm date).
