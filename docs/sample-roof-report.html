Summary

The roof is not leaking today and the deck and attic are dry. It has two small items that will leak within the year (findings 2 and 3) and storm damage on three facets that a buyer's inspector and any adjuster will find. The shingle is 14 years into a 30-year rating and the south and east facets have life left; the north and west do not. Left alone, I would put remaining life at 3 to 5 years before it needs replacement regardless of the hail.
