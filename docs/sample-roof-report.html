[photo: cracked pipe boot]

2. Cracked pipe boot, rear bathroom vent Fix soon

The rubber collar has split. Water gets in on the next heavy rain and shows up as a ceiling stain in the hall bath. A 20-minute repair.

[photo: chimney counter-flashing]

3. Chimney counter-flashing sealant failed Fix soon

Sealant has pulled away on the uphill side. No stain in the attic yet. Re-seal and re-set the counter-flashing.

[photo: lifted ridge cap]

4. Lifted ridge caps, east end Plan for it

Three ridge caps have lost their seal and lift in wind. Hand-seal now, or they go with a replacement.

[photo: south slope, good condition]
